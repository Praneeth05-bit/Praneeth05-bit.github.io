Dispute Court: Multi-Agent Card-Dispute Resolver ↗

A court of five AI agents (customer’s lawyer, merchant’s lawyer, fraud analyst, rule clerk, and judge) hears a card dispute and decides: refund, reject, or send to a human, with a plain-English letter to the customer. Small language models score every step with calibrated probabilities, and a 70B large language model writes the arguments. A case is decided in about 25 seconds, and uncertain ones escalate to a person instead of guessing.
